How to play

  • Get out with a 6. You need a 6 to move a token from the yard onto your start square.
  • Go around, then home. Tokens travel clockwise, then turn up your coloured column into the centre.
  • Safe squares. Stars and start squares protect every token on them.
  • Capture. Land on an opponent outside a safe square and they go back to their yard.
  • Extra roll for a 6, a capture, or getting a token home. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
  • Exact finish. You must roll the exact number to enter the centre.
